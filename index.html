Included Free · No Upsell

Real Resources You Keep — Taught Step-by-Step

Attendees don't just watch a demo. Every free resource is taught hands-on, step-by-step, until you can run it yourself. That includes free access to our investigative software suite.

Choose One · $600 Value Project V.I.P.E.R. Investigative platform · full access

Project V.I.P.E.R. is a modern investigative platform engineered specifically for small units and detective bureaus. With features like case-tracking dashboards, email and warrant parsing, and integrated third-party intelligence like LPRs, Project V.I.P.E.R. delivers the capabilities agencies have always needed but never had.

Choose One · $600 Value Project Oversight Sex offender management software · full access

We created Project Oversight for you. This sex offender management software is designed to handle all your daily tasks of managing your sex offender population — from registration to compliance checks and even multi-jurisdictional sweeps. Best part? It's completely free to attendees. Run it on your Windows PC or straight off a USB drive: all locally, all offline. No fees. Ever.

Included for Every Attendee Project Hindsight Digital triage tool · full access

Trying to keep up with technology is hard, and some of our offenders spend a lot of time trying to hide digital contraband from us. Project Hindsight is designed to help you locate that contraband on Windows computers and external drives such as USBs. Flag evidence, create reports, and let your forensic experts preserve the evidence. This is a true triage tool in every sense of the word — also completely free for attendees.

Every attendee leaves with Project Hindsight. You'll also select one free software license — Project V.I.P.E.R. or Project Oversight, a $600 value at no cost. As a rule of thumb, sworn officers choose V.I.P.E.R., while civilian staff responsible for the registry choose Oversight.

05 / The Instructors

Meet Your Instructors

Not names on a slide — practitioners who are still in the work. You learn directly from the people setting the standard in the field and the clinic.

JM

Justin Moyer

Founder, Intellect LE · 20+ Yrs CA Law Enforcement

K9 FTO Defensive Tactics Mobile Field Force ICAC Sex Offender Supervision

Justin has been a law enforcement officer in the State of California for over 20 years. Throughout his career he has worked numerous assignments such as K9, FTO, Defensive Tactics, Mobile Field Force, ICAC, and Sex Offender Supervision.

Justin has found a calling in training law enforcement from across the country in ICAC investigations and sex offender supervision, and has trained hundreds of officers over the past 5 years. He prides himself on locating and providing low-cost tools and resources to the law enforcement community, and has been a featured speaker at the Techno Data and Security Conference as well as the California Robbery Investigators Association Conference in 2023.

He is also the founder of Intellect LE, LLC and Inv-Network, whose mission is to provide training, tools, and news to the ICAC and sex offender supervision communities.

JW

Dr. Jennifer Weeks

Ph.D. · Founder & Director, Sexual Addiction Treatment Services

CSAT / Supervisor CAADC LPC CCSOTS Expert Witness

Dr. Jennifer Weeks is the founder and director of Sexual Addiction Treatment Services, an outpatient treatment program for out-of-control sexual behavior. She has almost 20 years' experience treating individuals who commit sex crimes and all aspects of addiction, trauma, and sexual addiction, assembling a staff of caring, compassionate professionals to help her clients heal.

She earned a bachelor's degree in Psychology from Purdue University and both her master's degree and Ph.D. in Psychology from The University of Georgia. She is a Certified Sexual Addiction Therapist/Supervisor, Certified Advanced Alcohol and Drug Counselor, Licensed Professional Counselor, and Clinically Certified Sex Offender Treatment Specialist.

A recognized expert witness in the forensic specialty of sex offenders and sexual compulsivity, she frequently provides expert testimony in both county and federal courts. Dr. Weeks is a faculty member of the Northeast Counterdrug Training Center Polygraph Program and Inv-Network, training law enforcement and probation/parole officers on sexual offending, and is an adjunct professor at Moravian University in the Graduate Clinical Counseling program.

She regularly provides continuing education to therapists and attorneys and presents at national conferences. She is the author of the blog Dr. Jen's Recovery Readings and the prevention book The New Age of Sex Education: How to Talk to Your Teen about Cybersex and Pornography in the Digital Age.
